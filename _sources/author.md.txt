# About the author

This repository is developed and maintained by Dr. Matteo Tomasini, senior assistant at the Institute of Archaeological Sciences at the University of Bern. Pytheas Simulator was developed when Dr. Tomasini worked at the [Gothenburg Research Infrastructure in Digital Humanities](https://dh.gu.se) within the project [Maritime Encounters](https://www.gu.se/en/research/maritime-encounters) at the University of Gothenburg. 

You can find more about him at his [personal webpage](https://mtomasini.github.io/).
